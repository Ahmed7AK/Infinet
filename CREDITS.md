### Blender-scripted (this project)

The other 33 GLBs in `public/models/` are built for this project by script in Blender 5.1 (`tools/blender/build_all.py`): access points, USB adapter, laptop, phone, 42U rack, 5G monopole, GEO dish, LEO terminal, site router, satellites, gateway, desks, meeting table, café counter, saloon, SUV and pickup (lofted bodies with separate wheels), palm, street lamp, bollard, bench, 20 ft container office, generator, office chair, bus shelter, litter bin, crossing sign, water cooler, printer, whiteboard and windsock. The office monitors and the meeting-room display show screens drawn at start-up (`src/app/scene/screens.ts`): bars and charts, no words or logos. Generic shapes only, no real product or brand. The network hardware stays scripted on purpose: downloadable models of these are almost always real products with logos.
