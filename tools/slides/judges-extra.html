What we'd hand an operator or a phone maker

Seven rules keep a moving call alive and private.

Value we use
Evidence Simulated · 300 runs per scene

3.4s earlyaverage lead before the Wi-Fi faded · 0 s frozen

Make before break
Send on two tunnels for 2 s around a switch, only where the delay meets the playout deadline
+2–4.4%extra data, by scene: the cost of the second copy

Never use an unchecked network
Screen, then probe in quarantine; timeout max(1 s, 3 × round trip); one flag caps at Tunnel only, two serious flags block
0 framesover a fake, unchecked or Blocked network in 5,000 runs

Every frame in the tunnel on every network; anchor down → nothing in the clear, call ends after 8 s
